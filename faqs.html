Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where are you located?

I am based in Hanford, CA and serve the surrounding local areas.

Do you speak any languages other than English?

Yes, I am fluent in both English and Tagalog.

How do payments work?

Payments are securely processed via Zelle, Venmo, or Cash upon completion of the service.

How can I request a service?

You can request services or ask questions by filling out the Contact Form on the Home page.

Do I need to be home when you provide services?

It depends on the service. For exterior yard maintenance, you do not need to be home as long as I have access to the property. For interior moving or cleaning services, you or a trusted representative must be present to provide access.

What happens if I need to cancel?

We request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. You can read more details on our Terms of Service page.
